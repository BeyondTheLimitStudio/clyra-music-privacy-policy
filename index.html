Privacy Policy

Developer: Beyond The Limit Studio
Effective Date: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Beyond The Limit Studio built the Clyra Music app as an ad-supported application with an optional in-app purchase upgrade (Clyra Premium). This service is provided by Beyond The Limit Studio and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use Clyra Music. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Clyra Music is designed primarily as an offline local music player: we do not operate private servers to upload, store, or sell your personal files or listening habits.

Key Promise: Clyra Music does not collect, upload, or transmit your songs, audio recordings, or personal listening library to any external server. All media playback and playlist management remain 100% on your device.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Local Audio Files & Media Storage (On-Device Only)

  • What is accessed: Audio files (MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, etc.), audio metadata (song titles, artist names, album titles, track durations), folder structures, and album art stored on your device.
  • How it is used: To index and display your music library, organize tracks by title, artist, album, and folder, create and manage custom playlists, and play music.
  • Data storage: All playlists, favorites, and equalizer preferences are stored locally in app-private storage.
  • No Uploads: Your audio files and metadata are never uploaded to any remote server or shared with third parties.

B. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

  • Clyra Music offers an optional one-time purchase ("Clyra Premium") to unlock custom accent colors and remove all advertisements.
  • Purchases and billing transactions are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing.
  • We do not collect or store credit card, debit card, or financial details. All transactions are securely handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • A cryptographically verified receipt token is kept locally on your device solely to maintain your active premium benefits.

C. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

  • For users of the free version, the app displays non-intrusive interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob.
  • Google AdMob may collect and process data in accordance with Google's policies, including:
    • Google Advertising ID (GAID / Ad ID)
    • Coarse/approximate geographic location (derived from IP address)
    • Device technical specifications (model, OS version, carrier)
    • Ad performance metrics and diagnostic error logs
  • Ad Frequency Capping: Clyra Music limits ad frequency to ensure an uninterrupted listening experience.
  • Clyra Premium: Once upgraded to Clyra Premium, all advertising SDK requests and interstitial ad loading are immediately and permanently disabled.

D. App Integrity & Anti-Tamper Verification (On-Device Only)

  • To protect the app from unauthorized modifications and fraudulent billing emulators, Clyra Music performs local integrity checks (e.g., verifying signature and installation origin).
  • These checks execute strictly on-device without uploading hardware serials, device fingerprints, or telemetry to any external server.

3. Permissions Requested & Purpose

Permission Purpose
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+) Access and index your local audio files for offline music playback.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below) Access local audio files on older Android versions (scoped to maxSdkVersion="32").
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK Allows continuous background audio playback with lock screen controls.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) Displays active playback notifications and media controls in the status bar.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents audio stuttering and keeps playback running when the screen turns off.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Loads advertisements (free tier only) and validates Google Play Store in-app purchases.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Required on Android 13+ for Google AdMob to provide compliant advertising services.
com.android.vending.BILLING Enables secure purchase and restoration of Clyra Premium via Google Play Store.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates services that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Data Collection
  • Google Play Terms of Service

5. User Data Control & Deletion

  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
  • Removing Ads: You can completely eliminate advertisements by purchasing Clyra Premium within the app.
  • Data Deletion: All app settings, playlists, and favorites are stored locally. You can permanently delete this data by going to Android Settings → Apps → Clyra Music → Storage & cache → Clear Storage or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Clyra Music does not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided personal information through our advertising partner, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Because Clyra Music does not transmit or store your personal library on external servers, your music and playlist data remain entirely within your device's protected app sandbox.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted.
